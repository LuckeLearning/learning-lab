The Present Simple – how to form it
Spelling Rules
(he / she / it)		AffirmativeNegativeQuestions
Most verbs: add “s”I eat an apple.
She eats an apple.		I don’t eat meat.
She doesn’t eat meat.		Do you eat fish?
Does she eat fish?
If the verb ends in sh or ch, add “es”We watch films.
He watches films.		We don’t watch films.
He doesn’t watch films.		Do they watch films?
Does he watch films?
If the verb ends in a consonant + y, omit “y” and add “ies”I study maths.
She studies maths.		I don’t study maths.
She doesn’t study maths.		Do you study maths?
Does she study maths?
If the verb ends in a vowel + y, just add “s”They play chess.
He plays chess.		They don’t play chess.
He doesn’t play chess.		Do they play chess?
Does he play chess?
Special verbs: have → has, do → does, go → goesI have a cat.
She has a cat.
He goes home.		She doesn’t have a cat.
He doesn’t go home.		Does she have a cat?
Does he go home?
Don’t forget! – do / does

After doesn’t and Does:
the verb has no “s”!		✓ He doesn’t play …   He doesn’t plays
✓ Does she like …?   Does she likes
Signal wordsalways, usually, often, sometimes, never → before the verb: Tom often reads comics.
every day, every morning … → at the end: She plays tennis every day.
